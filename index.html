Jancy's Fancies Custom Sewing and Personalization Studio

About Us

Jancy's Fancies Custom Sewing and Personalization Studio in Duncanville, Alabama, creates handmade treasures designed to make life's special moments even more meaningful. From baby quilts and blankets to nursery keepsakes, memory bears, and heirloom pieces, each creation is made to be loved and cherished for years.

The studio offers custom embroidery, monogramming, applique, personalized gifts, custom T-shirts, and business apparel for companies and teams. Customers can also bring their own hat patches or decorative clothing patches for professional sewing. Whether you need a ready-to-ship handmade item or a custom creation made for a special name, nursery, or milestone, Jancy's Fancies is here to help bring your ideas to life.

To explore available products and personalization services, visit the official Jancy's Fancies website.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What products does Jancy's Fancies offer?

Jancy's Fancies offers handmade baby quilts, blankets, nursery keepsakes, memory bears, personalized gifts, and other custom sewing creations.

2. Can I order personalized clothing and gifts?

Yes, the studio provides embroidery, monogramming, applique, custom T-shirts, and personalized apparel for individuals, companies, and teams.

Yes, customers can bring their own hat patches and decorative clothing patches for sewing and application.
